How to play

  1. Mount a blank. Pick an order and a timber. The blank spins on the lathe.
  2. Cut to the yellow line. The drawing is traced on the blank. Drag across it to peel the wood down. Aim nearer the centre to cut deeper.
  3. Pick the right tool. A roughing gouge hogs away bulk, a parting tool cuts crisp grooves, a detail carver does fine work.
  4. Don't overcut. Cutting below the line wastes wood and can't be undone. That's the skill (only the Rolling Pin order stops you).
  5. Sand, then oil. The sanding block smooths the piece; the oil brings out the grain and a soft shine.
  6. Finish. You're scored on shape, smoothness, symmetry, material use, finish and time.

Short, light passes give you the most control. There's no failing, only turning.
